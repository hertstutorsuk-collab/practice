Use the papers properly

Try a paper without checking the answers. Mark with the matching scheme, note where marks were lost, and take those questions to your tutor. Follow that paper’s calculator and timing instructions. Use grade boundaries for the correct board, tier and series; a practice score is not a predicted grade.
