    Make every paper count.

    1. Pick your tier. Ask your tutor if you are unsure whether you are studying Foundation or Higher.
    2. Try it first. Download the question paper and follow its printed instructions, time limit and equipment list.
    3. Mark and reflect. Use the matching mark scheme, note topics you missed, then practise those skills above.
